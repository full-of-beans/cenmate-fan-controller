# CENMATE fan controller PCB layout audit

**November 12, 2026 · Fabrication readiness — HOLD.** The latest layout has continuous copper and the intended pad-to-net assignments. The placement changes improve access to several passive groups, but they also increase the INA228 bypass loop and bring C5 closer to the screw terminal. Before ordering, reconcile the assembly data, qualify the actual keyed fan headers, and recheck the updated mounting pattern and connector envelopes against the backplate. No confirmed blocking copper, drill, or pin-to-net defect was found.

## Schematic-to-board parity and functional checks

All seven explicit no-connects match: J1.B, U1.3/.4/.7, U2.3 and U3.4/.5. The two 10 Ω component values and procurement metadata need reconciliation as in L26-01. The 10 Ω / 100 nF filter has a nominal differential cutoff of 79.6 kHz using `1/(2π(R3 + R4)C2)`. The PCB's stale 100 Ω value implies 7.96 kHz. Any substitution also needs firmware/register, measurement resolution and part-selection review. ## Routing, return paths and power integrity

The PCB changed from **247 to 240 track segments**, **760.1 to 835.4 mm total centerline length**, and **11 to 13 vias**. The fan 2 tach hop accounts for the two added transitions. The 12 V fan takeoff via moved, and the upstream sense via changed from 0.8/0.4 mm to 0.6/0.3 mm while remaining a measurement branch. The narrowest routed width is 0.25 mm on one front ground escape. U2's adjacent traces and pad spacing remain 0.20 mm at the limiting points. The existing U2 custom-rule name references INA228, consistent with the actual populated U2. Its reference-based width condition is not a blanket constraint for every segment on the sense nets.

## Mechanical and manufacturing review

**Board and installation.** Fresh board-only STEP and the committed assembly's PCB body both have one valid 30 × 80 × 1.51 mm core solid, about 3,544.95 mm³. Nominal board stack thickness is 1.6 mm. All four mount holes use 2.20 mm plated drills and 4.00 mm grounded pads. Each hole wall is nominally 1.60 mm from the profile after H4 shifted, giving 2.70 mm center-to-edge. A concentric 4 mm head envelope leaves 0.70 mm to the edge; check the actual washer, boss and seating tolerance. The side relief remains centered at (50.50, 112.10) with R3 concavity, R1 blends, and R2 outer corners. J3.1 is the nearest plated component hole to that cut at 1.173 mm from the hole wall.

**3D and protrusion.** Committed STEP placements for the local models agree with fresh exports after the centered origin translation. The loaded J1 model goes 14.09 mm below the core reference and its terminals protrude to Z=+3.72 mm. The J2 model projects to Z=+11.60 mm and its tails to Z=−1.91 mm. The Molex header models reach Z=+7.77 mm and Z=−2.16 mm; the two TO-92 models from the committed assembly extend to Z=−3.41 mm before lead trimming. The J1 model's lead sections align with the slots within 0.05 mm in XY; the J2 lead-center differences are +0.01/+0.05 mm from the holes. J2's modeled insertion footprint remains plausible. RSH1 and the two thermistor STEP files are package proxies. J3/J4 modeled front-to-back bounds leave only about 0.90 mm to the edge at Y=129 mm before mating. U1's USB end reaches roughly Y=49.19 mm, near the opposite edge. These dimensions establish the board/model geometry. The actual backplate, cables, screw stack, plug envelopes and assembled tolerances were unavailable for independent fit verification. L26-03 and L26-08 track those limits.

**XIAO underside.** The active footprint has 14 edge contacts and no copper pads for the 16 underside contacts. Overlaying all 16 fabrication markers on plotted front mask showed no mask openings at those contact envelopes and a nearest opening gap of about 0.76 mm. Several marked contact envelopes overlap copper beneath the mask. Both sides of all 13 vias are tented in CAM. Inspect physical mask coverage and module seating during first-article assembly, particularly because the model intersects the board in Z.

## Prioritized remediation

### Before fabrication release

1. Reconcile R3/R4 and U2 in the board and assembly data. Freeze the actual U2/J2/fan-header configuration, then rerun ERC, DRC with refilled zones and parity, and native export comparison.
2. Verify the actual keyed fan connector and plug against the drill, pinout and mated clearance. Check H4 and J2 against the latest panel and full harness, including USB, J1 and the standoff notch.
3. Confirm current Gerber/drill geometry in vendor CAM for green 1 oz, the notch, tented vias, Q1/Q2 webs, plated slots and critical legends. If using PCBA, approve BOM, CPL, rotations, board sides and excluded parts together.

### Before connecting drives for normal operation

1. Verify polarity, ground continuity and USB/12 V power sequencing with a current-limited source and unloaded or controlled-load outputs. Establish brick fault handling and the behavior of the fans when USB is lost with 12 V present.
2. Check fan startup, PWM inversion, tach waveforms and maximum-duty operation. Measure U3 VIN and U2 VS during switched load and check thermistor bias with warm hardware and moving air.
3. Calibrate the current monitor and compare fixed HDD-load readings with the fans toggled. Under the accepted J1 prototype disposition, measure total current, contact drop and temperature at six-drive cold startup and sustained load. Evaluate the measured envelope against the jack and harness limits.

### Improvements if justified by the build or measurements

Shorten the C1-to-U2 bypass loop, improve C5 access if tool clearance is inadequate, and revisit the fan tach or TH2 routing if noise is measurable. Align the package models before relying on them for automated enclosure clearance checks.
