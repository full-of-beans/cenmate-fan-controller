## Copper paths and assembly orientation

- The B.Cu HDD feed preserves 4 mm strokes from J1.A to RSH1.2 and from RSH1.1 to J2.1. J1.C and J2.2 join one connected B.Cu GND plane with no detached pour island in the plot. Q and fan GND connections are present. The B.Cu CAM image shows thermals at J3.1 and J4.1 and broad connections at J1.C and J2.2. The independent net check also ties U3.2 and all four mounting pads to ground.
- The separate shunt sense branches leave their respective current lands through vias to the INA228 input networks. This preserves the intended split takeoffs on the two-terminal shunt, with shared land resistance still subject to calibration. U3 VIN connects to upstream 12 V, VOUT to both fan supply pins and GND to the return. The exposed J2 pad polarity agrees with the front legends, left GND at J2.2 and right 12V at J2.1 viewed from the front.
- The bottom J1 box and dot orient the jack around its three distinctive slots. U1 has the USB end outline and two dots near its pin-1 end. U2 has a triangle at its pin-1 end. U3 uses the longer outline stroke on the pin-1 end rather than a dedicated dot. Q1/Q2 retain the D-shaped body outline and rectangular pad 1. J3/J4 have legible references and rectangular body outlines but no dedicated silk pin-1 mark. Pin numbering is 1 GND, 2 switched 12 V, 3 tach, 4 PWM. Identify the GND end from the assembly drawing or by continuity before installing the physical header.

## Reproduction and release

From a clean checkout, using KiCad 10.0.8 and its matching standard libraries:

```bash
python3 -m venv /tmp/cenmate-cam-venv
/tmp/cenmate-cam-venv/bin/pip install gerbonara==1.6.3 shapely==2.2.0
out=$(mktemp -d)
scripts/export-pcb-manufacturing.sh "$out/cam"
/tmp/cenmate-cam-venv/bin/python scripts/inspect-pcb-manufacturing.py \
  "$out/cam" hardware/fan-controller.kicad_pcb | tee "$out/measurements.txt"
p=hardware/fan-controller
kicad-cli sch erc --format json --exit-code-violations -o "$out/erc.json" "$p.kicad_sch"
kicad-cli pcb drc --format json --schematic-parity --refill-zones \
  --exit-code-violations -o "$out/drc.json" "$p.kicad_pcb"
```

Set `KICAD_CLI` to the full path to the KiCad 10 launcher if needed. The inspector writes per-layer SVGs beside the Gerbers. It reports measured clearances for review and checks this board's layer inventory, profile topology, drill registration and source dimensions, pad/net inventory, track-width inventory and net continuity. Polygonization and drill rounding limit numerical precision. Gerbonara warns about the placement of KiCad's Excellon `G90` after the header, then parses the expected absolute coordinates, which were independently matched to source. These checks cover the supplied artwork and assume the plotted holes are plated as designated. They do not quantify etch tolerances, arbitrary same-net neck widths or actual plating. Legend coverage after parts are installed was assessed from the footprint and artwork, with physical readability still open.

Before ordering, use the regenerated plot and populated PTH file in JLCPCB CAM. Confirm 30 × 80, two layers, the relief and corner routing, all three plated J1 slots and four plated M2 holes, Q1/Q2 webs, via tenting and critical markings. Check the real MODDIY connector against a 1:1 print or sample and verify the keyed pinout. The audit made no layout corrections and generated no production ZIP.
